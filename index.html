How to play

Start each campaign by choosing a perk and selecting your captain. Build your squad with player cards, then improve them through training.

During matches, choose attacking and defensive actions. Each action has odds affected by your players, their abilities and the opposition.

Win fixtures, collect rewards and work towards becoming champions of the North West.
